Money Tools

FreeLifeTools includes calculators for common money questions such as estimating pay, organizing a monthly budget, evaluating rent, planning debt payments and reviewing credit utilization.

Start with income

Knowing approximately how much you earn can make other calculations easier. The paycheck tools estimate gross income, while your actual pay statements can help you identify your take-home income for budgeting.

Compare income with expenses

After reviewing income, list regular expenses such as housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance and debt payments. This can provide a clearer picture of how much money remains for savings and other goals.

Why FreeLifeTools?

Many everyday questions start with a simple calculation: What could my paycheck be? How much am I spending? How long could debt take to repay? What rent might fit my budget?

FreeLifeTools brings those types of calculators and practical resources together in one place. Each major tool includes an explanation of what it calculates, examples and limitations so you can understand the result rather than receiving only a number.

Free Tools vs. Professional Advice

FreeLifeTools is designed for general educational and planning purposes. Calculator results are estimates and are not financial, tax, legal, credit, employment or other professional advice.

For decisions requiring individualized advice, consider using an appropriate qualified professional or official source.
