About FreeLifeTools

Free tools for money, jobs, AI and everyday life.

Why FreeLifeTools Exists

FreeLifeTools was created to make useful everyday tools easier to access. Managing money, looking for work and organizing everyday responsibilities can be complicated. Our goal is to make those tasks simpler with practical tools that are easy to understand and use.

Instead of requiring complicated spreadsheets or expensive software, FreeLifeTools brings helpful calculators, planners and resources together in one place.

Our Goal

Our goal is to continue building useful tools that solve real everyday problems. We focus on keeping the website simple, mobile-friendly and easy to navigate.
