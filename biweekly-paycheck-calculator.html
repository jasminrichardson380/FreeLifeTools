How is biweekly pay calculated?

Biweekly pay means you are normally paid once every two weeks. That usually equals 26 paychecks per year.

This calculator multiplies your hourly wage by your weekly hours and then multiplies that amount by two to estimate your biweekly gross paycheck.

These results are before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, and other paycheck deductions.

Example: $17 an hour paid biweekly

If you make $17 an hour and work 40 hours per week, your estimated gross pay is $680 per week and $1,360 every two weeks.

Your estimated annual gross income would be $35,360 before taxes and deductions.
