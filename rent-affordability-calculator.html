How much rent can you afford?

A common budgeting guideline is to keep rent around 30% of gross monthly income. This is only a general guideline and may not fit every household or housing market.

This calculator also gives a more conservative estimate by taking your monthly debt payments into account.

Example: $3,000 monthly income

If your gross income is $3,000 per month, 30% of your income is $900. If you also have significant monthly debt payments, a lower rent target may give you more room for groceries, transportation, savings, utilities, and emergencies.
