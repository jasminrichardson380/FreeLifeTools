Resume Writing Tips

A strong resume makes your relevant experience easier for an employer to understand quickly.

Match the Job

Read the job description and emphasize truthful skills and experience that are relevant to that position.

Use Clear Language

Keep descriptions direct and easy to scan. Avoid adding unnecessary wording that hides your actual experience.

Show Results When Real

If you have accurate numbers or measurable results, consider including them. Never invent statistics just to make a resume sound stronger.

How to Write a Resume

1. Add your contact information

Include appropriate contact information so an employer can reach you. Check your phone number and email address for accuracy before submitting applications.

2. Write a focused professional summary

A professional summary can briefly explain your relevant experience, strengths and the type of work you are prepared to do.

Example structure:

Customer service professional with experience assisting customers, documenting account information and resolving routine service concerns. Skilled in communication, organization and working in fast-paced environments.

3. Describe your work experience

List relevant jobs and explain your responsibilities using clear action-oriented language. Focus on what you actually did rather than vague descriptions.

4. Add relevant skills

Include skills that accurately reflect your abilities and are useful for the positions you are applying for.

5. Add education and certifications

Include relevant education, training, licenses or certifications. Follow the employer's instructions when a position requires specific credentials.

6. Proofread before applying

Check spelling, grammar, dates, job titles and contact information. Make sure the final resume accurately reflects your background.

Before You Submit Your Resume

A short final review can help catch mistakes before an employer receives your application.

Check Contact Details

Make sure your phone number and email address are current and typed correctly.

Review the Job Posting

Confirm that your resume highlights the experience and skills you actually have that relate to the position.

Save a Clean Copy

Keep a copy of the version you submitted so you know what the employer received if you are contacted for an interview.

Resume Helper FAQ

Is the FreeLifeTools Resume Helper free?

Yes. You can use the resume tools on this page without purchasing a FreeLifeTools Pro subscription.

Does this tool automatically submit job applications?

No. It helps organize resume wording and prompts. You remain responsible for reviewing your resume and submitting your applications.

Does this tool contact an AI service?

No. The browser-based helper on this page organizes the information you enter into structured prompts. It does not independently send your information to an external AI service.

Should I use the exact same resume for every job?

You can keep a main resume, but it may help to adjust the emphasis for different positions so the most relevant truthful experience is easier to find.

Can I put skills on my resume that I am still learning?

Make sure your wording accurately represents your current level of experience. Avoid presenting a developing skill as advanced expertise if that is not accurate.
