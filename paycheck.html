How the Paycheck Calculator Works

The calculator starts with your hourly wage and the number of hours you work during an average week. It multiplies those numbers to estimate your weekly gross income.

That weekly amount is then used to estimate your biweekly, twice-monthly, monthly and annual earnings.

Example

If you earn $19 per hour and work 40 hours per week:

$19 × 40 hours = $760 gross pay per week.

A standard biweekly estimate would be $1,520 before taxes and other deductions.

Weekly vs. Biweekly vs. Twice-Monthly Pay

Weekly pay

Weekly employees are generally paid once each week. A full year normally contains 52 weekly pay periods.

Biweekly pay

Biweekly employees are generally paid every two weeks. This usually results in 26 paychecks during a full year.

Twice-monthly pay

Twice-monthly, or semimonthly, employees are normally paid two times each month. That generally results in 24 paychecks per year.

Biweekly and twice-monthly pay schedules are not the same. A biweekly schedule is based on 26 pay periods per year, while a twice-monthly schedule generally has 24.

Gross Pay vs. Take-Home Pay

Gross pay is the amount you earn before payroll deductions. Take-home pay is the amount that remains after applicable taxes, insurance premiums, retirement contributions and other deductions.

Because deductions vary from person to person, this calculator estimates gross income rather than promising an exact take-home paycheck amount.

Common paycheck deductions may include:

  • Federal income tax withholding
  • Social Security tax
  • Medicare tax
  • State or local income taxes where applicable
  • Health insurance premiums
  • Retirement plan contributions
  • Other voluntary or required deductions

Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Is this paycheck calculator free?

Yes. You can use the FreeLifeTools paycheck calculator without paying to calculate your estimated gross income.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator estimates gross pay before taxes and payroll deductions.

How many biweekly paychecks are there in a year?

A standard biweekly pay schedule generally produces 26 paychecks during a full year.

Why is monthly pay different from four weekly paychecks?

A year has 52 weeks. Dividing 52 weeks by 12 months gives an average of about 4.33 weeks per month, so an average monthly estimate is usually higher than simply multiplying weekly pay by four.

Are the results guaranteed to match my paycheck?

No. The results are estimates for educational and planning purposes. Your actual paycheck can vary because of overtime, unpaid time, taxes, benefits and other payroll deductions.
