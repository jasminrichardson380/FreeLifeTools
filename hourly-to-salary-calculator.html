How do you convert hourly pay to salary?

Multiply your hourly wage by the number of hours you work each week. Then multiply that amount by the number of weeks you work each year.

For example, someone earning $17 an hour and working 40 hours per week for 52 weeks would earn an estimated $35,360 per year before taxes and deductions.

Hourly pay examples

At 40 hours per week, $15 an hour is about $31,200 per year, $17 an hour is about $35,360 per year, and $20 an hour is about $41,600 per year before taxes.
