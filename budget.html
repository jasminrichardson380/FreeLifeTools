How to Use the Monthly Budget Calculator

Start with the amount of money you normally receive after payroll deductions. Then enter your regular monthly expenses.

The calculator adds your expenses together and subtracts them from your income so you can quickly see whether your current plan leaves money available at the end of the month.

Example monthly budget

Take-home income: $3,200

Total expenses: $2,650

Money remaining: $550

The remaining amount could be used for additional savings, debt payments, irregular expenses or other financial goals.

What Should You Include in a Monthly Budget?

Housing

Include rent or mortgage payments along with utilities and other recurring housing expenses.

Food

Include groceries as well as any regular restaurant or takeout spending you want to account for.

Transportation

Transportation may include a vehicle payment, gas, insurance, public transportation, rideshare costs or other commuting expenses.

Debt payments

Include credit cards, personal loans and other regular debt payments in your monthly plan.

Savings

Savings can be treated like another planned monthly expense. Setting aside a specific amount can make it easier to work toward emergency savings or other financial goals.

What If Your Expenses Are Higher Than Your Income?

If the calculator shows a negative amount, your planned expenses are higher than the income entered. That can be a useful signal to review the numbers more closely.

Start by checking whether every amount is accurate. Then separate expenses that are required from expenses that may be adjustable. Even small changes across several categories can affect the final monthly total.

Monthly Budget FAQ

Is this budget calculator free?

Yes. You can use this monthly budget calculator for free.

Should I enter gross income or take-home income?

For a spending budget, take-home income is often more useful because it represents the money available after payroll deductions.

What if my income changes each month?

You can use a conservative estimate based on a lower-income month or recalculate the budget whenever your expected income changes.

Should savings be included as an expense?

You can include planned savings as part of your monthly budget. This helps show how much money remains after both expenses and the amount you want to save.

Does FreeLifeTools save my budget information?

This calculator performs the calculation in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive account numbers, passwords or other private financial information into online calculators.
