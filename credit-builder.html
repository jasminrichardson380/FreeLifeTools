What Is Credit Utilization?

Credit utilization compares the balance reported on revolving credit accounts, such as credit cards, with the amount of revolving credit available.

For a single card, you can estimate utilization by dividing the card balance by its credit limit and multiplying the result by 100.

Example

If a credit card has a $500 balance and a $2,000 credit limit, the estimated utilization on that card is 25%.

Credit scoring models may consider utilization along with payment history, account age, credit mix, new credit activity and other information in a credit report.

Practical Credit-Building Basics

Pay bills by their due dates

Keeping track of due dates can help you avoid missed payments and late fees. Automatic payments or reminders may be useful when available.

Review your credit reports

Reviewing your credit reports can help you identify accounts you do not recognize or information you believe is inaccurate. Follow the credit bureau's dispute process when appropriate.

Be careful with new debt

Opening new accounts simply to try to increase a score can create additional costs or debt. Consider the fees, interest rates and terms before applying.

Reduce balances when your budget allows

Paying down revolving balances may lower utilization, but avoid using money needed for essential expenses just to reach a particular utilization percentage.

Credit Utilization FAQ

Does 30% utilization guarantee a good credit score?

No. Credit scores use multiple factors. A utilization percentage by itself does not determine your entire score.

Is 10% utilization required?

No. The calculator includes 10% as a planning reference, not as a requirement or guarantee.

Will paying my credit card immediately raise my score?

Not necessarily. The timing of balance reporting varies, and a score can be affected by other information in your credit report.

Does FreeLifeTools report payments to credit bureaus?

No. This calculator is an educational planning tool. FreeLifeTools does not report your calculator entries or payments to credit bureaus.

Does this calculator store my card information?

This calculator does not ask for your credit card number, bank login or Social Security number. Only enter the numerical amounts needed for the calculation.
