Simple Ways to Manage Money

Track your spending

Knowing where your money goes can make it easier to identify expenses you may be able to reduce.

Create a realistic budget

Start with your income and regular expenses. Leave room for savings, unexpected costs and occasional spending.

Build an emergency fund

Even small, consistent savings can help create a cushion for unexpected expenses.

Understand your debt

Review balances, interest rates and minimum payments so you can decide which debts to prioritize.
